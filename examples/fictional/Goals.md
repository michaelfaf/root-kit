## The vision

Ridgeline becomes the grounds vendor a property manager never has to think about — one account manager per property, crews that show up on schedule, quality problems fixed before the renewal call. Dana's role shrinks from "does every job on the truck" to "sets the standard and reads the numbers" — estimating and site walks move to Wes and the crew leads, and Dana's daily time goes to client relationships and growth decisions instead of execution.

**W-2 crews, no subcontracted mowing:** locked permanently, not a phase — see `Business.md` §8. Every growth plan below assumes this holds.

## Cadence — the minimum that happens no matter what

**Dana's daily minimum: 3 client touches** — a renewal conversation, a site visit, or an enhancement proposal counts. Doesn't count: internal ops calls, scheduling, or anything with Marisol or Wes.
- Roughly 45–60 minutes a day, protected on the calendar before anything else gets booked.
- Logged in Aspire against the account, so it's checkable, not self-reported.

**Cadence:**
- **Daily:** 3 client touches (Dana).
- **Weekly:** Monday ops meeting (crews, scheduling, quality) · Friday renewal review (Wes, contract status by tier).
- **Monthly/Quarterly:** financial review with the bookkeeper; quarterly priorities refresh (this file).

**Accountability:** if the daily touches slip for more than three days running, or a Q3 priority above hasn't moved in two weeks, say so directly before moving to anything else in the session.

## 2029 picture

**Financial:** $6M revenue.
**Model:** still 100% W-2 crews, no subcontracted labor — unchanged from today.
**Measurable:** roughly 1,000 properties under contract across three branches.

**What it looks like:**

Three branches — Fort Collins, Greeley, Loveland — each with its own account manager and crew leads, run to the same standard because the standard is written down and audited the same way everywhere, not because Dana is checking each one personally.

Dana is out of estimating entirely. Her week is strategy, branch reviews, and the relationships on the handful of Anchor accounts she still wants to own personally, by choice rather than necessity.

**Structure:**
- Dana — strategy, Anchor relationships, branch oversight
- Marisol — regional operations, over all three branches' crews
- Wes — sales lead, training an account manager per branch

**What we don't do:**
- Subcontracted crews, at any branch, at any margin.
- Residential accounts.
