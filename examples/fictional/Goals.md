## Cadence — the minimum that happens no matter what

**Dana's daily minimum: 3 client touches** — a renewal conversation, a site visit, or an enhancement proposal. Not ops calls, scheduling, or anything with Marisol or Wes. Roughly 45–60 minutes, protected on the calendar first. Logged in Aspire against the account, so it's checkable, not self-reported.

**Cadence:** Daily — 3 client touches (Dana). Weekly — Monday ops meeting (crews, scheduling, quality), Friday renewal review (Wes, by tier). Monthly/quarterly — financial review with the bookkeeper; priorities refresh (this file).

**Accountability:** if the daily touches slip more than three days running, or a Q3 priority hasn't moved in two weeks, say so directly before anything else.

## 2029 picture

**Financial:** $6M revenue. **Model:** still 100% W-2 crews, no subcontracted labor. **Measurable:** roughly 1,000 properties across three branches.

**What it looks like:**

Three branches — Fort Collins, Greeley, Loveland — each with its own account manager and crew leads, run to the same standard because the standard is written down and audited everywhere, not because Dana checks each one personally.

Dana is out of estimating entirely. Her week is strategy, branch reviews, and the Anchor relationships she still wants to own personally, by choice rather than necessity.

**Structure:**
- Dana — strategy, Anchor relationships, branch oversight
- Marisol — regional operations, over all three branches' crews
- Wes — sales lead, training an account manager per branch

**What we don't do:**
- Subcontracted crews, at any branch, at any margin.
- Residential accounts.
