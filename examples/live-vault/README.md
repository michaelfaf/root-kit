# Live vault — two real root files

`CLAUDE.md` and `Home.md`, copied unedited from the Obsidian vault this kit was extracted from. A small B2B services business, run daily with Claude Code.

**What to notice:**

- **The router is under 1,700 words** and does an enormous amount of routing. Almost every line points somewhere. Count how few facts it actually contains.
- **The routing table is written in the owner's words** — "is this me being dumb", "what would the CFO think", "grab the prompt I drafted". Not categories. Intents, phrased the way he'd actually type them.
- **The tripwires are two bullets.** Out of an entire business, two facts made it into the always-loaded file: how the labor cost is classified, and which work isn't chargeable by design. Everything else routes to the business file. That's the discipline.
- **The editing rules are graded**, not binary — a typo gets fixed silently, a rewrite needs a sign-off, infrastructure always asks. This is the section most people skip and then wonder why their AI is either timid or reckless.
- **The map holds a depth-2 ceiling.** Folders and load-bearing files. No leaf notes anywhere, even though the vault has thousands.
- **It carries local conventions you don't have** — named skills, a CRM, a specific project structure. Those are the wiring. Ignore them; the shape is the mechanism.

**What to notice about the mess:** there are pointers to files you can't see, an odd naming scheme with number prefixes, and at least one convention that clearly exists because something once went wrong. That's what a root file looks like after real use. Yours will not be clean either, and it shouldn't be.
