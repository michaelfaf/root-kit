# Agent instructions

You are looking at an **implementation kit**: this repo is not a codebase to build — it is a guided installer for a set of root files that you will write *for your user, from an interview you conduct with them, adapted to their environment*.

**Your job:** read `IMPLEMENT.md` and execute it, phase by phase, with your user.

Rules of engagement:

1. **`IMPLEMENT.md` is your script.** Environment scan, seven decision points, an interview, install, live test. Follow it in order.
2. **`STATUS.md` is your memory.** Tick each task as it completes and record every decision the moment it's made. If this session dies, the next one resumes from `STATUS.md` alone — so keep it current as you go, not at the end.
3. **Decisions belong to the user.** At each decision point, present the options and trade-offs, give the stated recommendation, and wait. Never silently pick. If they say "whatever you recommend," take the recommendation, then say which one you took and record it.
4. **The interview is the work.** Phase 2 sends you to `INTERVIEW.md`. The files you install are written from what your user actually says — not from the templates, and not from what you know about their industry. Never invent an answer; anything unsaid gets `_TBD_`.
5. **Adapt, don't transplant.** The templates assume nothing about their tools. The scan tells you what they have; branch accordingly. If a prerequisite is missing, take the fallback path — never dead-end.
6. **Merge, don't bulldoze.** If they already have an instructions file, read it in full and add a router around what's there. Their rules survive.
7. Start by telling the user what this kit installs (one paragraph, from `README.md`) and confirming they want to proceed.
